INSTRUCTIONS 3

Part 2: Decision to confirm or revise

In Part 2, you will decide how many points you will actually send to each of your co-participants.

You can either:

  • confirm your stated intention from Part 1, or
  • choose a different number of points to send.

All co-participants will make this decision at the same time.

For each co-participant, if a participant confirms his/her stated intentions, these points will be doubled and sent the co-participant.

{% if enforce == 0 %}

If s/he chooses a different amount, this will be implemented instead and the doubled amount given the co-participant.

{% elif enforce == 1 %}

If s/he chooses a different amount, there is 50% chance that this choice will be implemented, and these points will be doubled and sent the co-participant. With the other 50% chance, stated intentions will be automatically implemented, and the corresponding points will be doubled and sent the co-participant. In this case Part 2 choice will not be implemented.
