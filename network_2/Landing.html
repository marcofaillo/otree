Welcome, and thank you for taking part in this activity.

During the activity you will interact with other participants living in the same country as you.

If you complete this activity, you will earn a fixed amount of £4 , plus an additional sum. That additional sum will depend on your choices and on the choices of another participant, who you will be randomly matched with.

After reading the instructions, you will be asked to answer some control questions to make sure you understand the rules.

If you answer one or more of these questions incorrectly on your first attempt, you will receive feedback indicating which answers were incorrect.

You will then have the opportunity to try again. The activity will only proceed once all participants in this session have answered all the questions correctly.

Please wait a few seconds for a button to appear; you can then click it to proceed.
